### Awesome Copilot workflows

Ready-made agentic workflow examples include `daily-issues-report`, `ospo-contributors-report`, `ospo-org-health`, `ospo-release-compliance-checker`, `ospo-stale-repos`, `relevance-check`, `relevance-summary`, and `weekly-comment-sync`.
